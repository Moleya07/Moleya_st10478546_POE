Our Story

The Good Foundation dubbed 'The Beacon of Hope' was born from a the suburbs of Louis Trichardt founded by Dr Penniel M. His love and compassion for the less fortunate, vulnerable and the needy was the reason all hands came together in the community to partake in this great initiative.
