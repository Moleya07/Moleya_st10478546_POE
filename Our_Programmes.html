Our Programmes

Explore our range of upcoming programmes designed to uplift our community.

Food Distribution Drive

The Mission: Tackling food insecurity by preparing warm, hearty meals and distributing nutritious food parcels to families in need.

The Annual School Shoe Drive

The Mission: Ensuring every child can attend school with confidence by distributing quality school shoes and essential uniform items to underprivileged learners.

Career & Academic Development Workshop

The Mission: Equipping youth for a brighter future through career mentorship, university application assistance, and academic tutoring services.

Mandela Day Outreach

The Mission: Celebrating the legacy of Nelson Mandela by spending our 67 minutes cooking meals, distributing warm blankets, and providing care at a local facility for the elderly.
